ShadrackTrades update
- Added major forex pairs and common indices to the new-trade form, edit form, and trade-log filter.
- Trade Log now sorts newest-recorded trades first.
- New and edited trades store createdAt/updatedAt timestamps for stable ordering and conflict resolution.
